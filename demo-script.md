# 2-Minute Demo Script

## Opening

“Large sporting venues have a simple but painful problem: people don’t just need directions — they need the **best decision right now**.

Smart Crowd Navigator is a Gemini-powered stadium assistant that tells attendees **where to go, whether to go now or wait, and which route will save the most time**.”

## Persona

“Our user is the group coordinator — the person in a family or friend group who decides where everyone should go for food, washrooms, entry, and exit.”

## Demo Flow

### 1. Set context

“Here the user is sitting in Section A.”

### 2. Ask for food

“Instead of searching a map manually, they tap **Food**.”

### 3. Show recommendation

“The assistant checks live queue data, crowd conditions, and route cost, then responds:

‘Wait 6 minutes, then go to Stall B.  
ETA: 4 minutes.  
Queue: 3 minutes.  
This avoids the current food rush near your section and saves about 8 minutes overall.’”

### 4. Explain the intelligence

“This is the key difference: the system doesn’t just answer *where*. It also answers *whether now is the right time to move*.”

### 5. Trigger live change

“Now I update the live venue state from the operator console.”

### 6. Show new outcome

“The assistant updates and now recommends:

‘Go now to Stall C. Crowd pressure has dropped, and this is now the fastest option.’”

### 7. Second scenario

“The same logic also works for washrooms, entry gates, and exit routing.”

## Close

“So this solution improves the physical event experience by reducing congestion, reducing waiting time, and helping people make smarter movement decisions in real time — using Gemini, Firebase, Cloud Run, and Google Maps in a practical way.”
